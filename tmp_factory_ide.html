Key Features

Don't switch your IDE

Works natively everywhere: VSCode (and its forks), JetBrains, Vim, and more. Don't force your team to switch IDEs, Factory meets developers in their preferred environment.

Feature-rich terminal experience

Slash commands, custom subagents, native diff viewing, context tagging, and more. You'll be surprised how much we packed into a simple and snappy experience.

Adjustable autonomy levels

Fine-tune how independently your agent operates. Allow certain changes automatically while requiring approval for others. From fully supervised to autonomous execution, you set the boundaries.
